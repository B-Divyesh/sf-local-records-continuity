Privacy policy

How Continuity Pack handles data

Effective 30 August 2026

Continuity Pack keeps business records under your control. The CLI has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or background network requests. It reads configured paths and writes a pack only to the target you name.

Data on your device

The CLI creates encrypted .cpack files, readable manifests, and JSON receipts. Passphrases can come from a file, environment variable, hidden prompt, or Linux Secret Service. We do not receive these files or passphrases.

License data

This site stores your Plus license token and last result in browser storage. It sends the token to Sociobot only for verification or a requested download. The token is removed from the visible URL after checkout. Paid files are not stored in the public site cache.

The download endpoint stores a one-way hash of the license token or network address. It also stores one-byte request counters in Azure Blob Storage. Each fixed one-minute window has a separate counter. No business record data is sent.

Purchases

Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record. It processes payment, tax, receipt, and refund information under its own policies. This site never receives card details.

Analytics and third parties

This version uses no analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or third-party runtime scripts. Sociobot requests happen only when you verify a license or ask to buy Plus. The purchase action checks checkout availability before leaving this site.

Removal and contact

Clear this site’s storage in your browser to remove its saved license. On Linux, use continuity key forget to remove the Secret Service entry. For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
