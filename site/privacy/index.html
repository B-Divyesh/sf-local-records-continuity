Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026

Continuity Pack is designed to keep your business records under your control. The CLI has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or background network requests. It reads only the paths you configure and copies a pack only to the target you name.

Data on your device

The CLI creates encrypted .cpack files, readable manifest text files, and JSON receipts. A passphrase can come from a file, environment variable, hidden prompt, or your operating system keychain. We do not receive these files or passphrases.

License data

If you buy or restore Continuity Plus, this site stores the license token and last verification result in your browser’s local storage. It sends that token to the Sociobot API to verify the license and to this site’s protected download endpoint when you request a field-kit file. The token is removed from the visible URL after checkout. Paid files are not stored in the public site cache. No business record data is sent.

Purchases

Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and processes payment, tax, receipt, and refund information under its own policies. This site never receives card details.

Analytics and third parties

This version uses no analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or third-party runtime scripts. Optional Sociobot requests happen only when you verify a license or ask to buy Plus; the latter checks whether checkout is enabled before navigating. No business record data is sent.

Removal and contact

Clear this site’s storage in your browser to remove its saved license. Use continuity key forget to remove the CLI keychain entry. For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
