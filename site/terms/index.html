What the tool verifies

A successful check means the selected pack authenticated, decrypted, and contained files matching its recorded checksums. It does not prove that your application can import those files, that every business record was exported, or that the source system is recoverable. You should regularly perform a full restore drill in a safe environment.

Your responsibilities

  • Choose the correct source exports and confirm they are current.
  • Protect and retain your passphrase separately from the pack.
  • Use a target with sufficient space and appropriate physical security.
  • Review scheduled failures and test application-level restoration.
